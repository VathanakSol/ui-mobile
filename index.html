      One flow, timed
      to the millisecond

      “Hold to unlock” is the product's moment of truth. Here it is decomposed into gesture, haptic, network and animation lanes so nobody has to guess at implementation.

      Step 01

      Gesture begins

      Touch-down on the unlock control starts a 600 ms hold timer and fills the progress ring.

      t 0 ms · cancel on slop > 10 dp
      Step 02

      Tactile tick

      Rigid impact fires at 30 % fill so the user knows the hold registered before anything network-bound happens.

      t 180 ms · iOS .rigid / Android EFFECT_TICK
      Step 03

      Command dispatched

      BLE write to the lock, mirrored to the server. Optimistic row inserted into the trip ledger immediately.

      t 600 ms · timeout 3 s · retry ×2 backoff
      Step 04

      Acknowledged

      Success haptic, button morphs to check, sheet collapses to peek, HUD route fades in underneath.

      t ≤950 ms p95 · budget 1 200 ms
      Step 05

      Transition

      Shared-element zoom from the bike marker into the HUD card. Map camera eases to the rider, 24 m altitude.

      t +420 ms · spring d 0.72
      Lane timing · p50 in grey, p95 ceiling in colour
      Budget guardrails: Frame ≤ 16.7 ms at 60 Hz Jank < 1 % of frames Tap→next-frame < 100 ms Cold start → interactive 1.8 s

        One design, two dialects. Everything not listed here is identical by construction — the delta is deliberately short, and it is the only place a designer and an engineer are allowed to disagree in writing.

        Accessibility is a
        build gate, not a page

        Contrast ratios in this document are computed from the actual hex values at runtime. Screen-reader scripts are the literal strings QA will listen for, in order.

        Spoken order — Discover screen

        VoiceOver / TalkBack parity
        1. “VELO, map view. 6 bikes within 200 metres. Search destination, text field.”
        2. Filters, collection view, 4 of 7 selected — trait: selected
        3. Kastanienhub, 40 metres ahead, 6 bikes docked — heading level 2
        4. VELO-2291, e-bike, battery 78 percent, 24 kilometres range, button
        5. Hold to unlock, button — hint: “hold for one second” · value: “unlocks in 1 second”
        Never expose the raw ID alone. “VELO-2291” reads as letters-and-digits; the accessible label is "bike ending 2 2 9 1" and the visual label stays monospaced.

        Handoff rules
        how this doc stays true
        
          
        
            
        Source of truth is the token file. Figma variables are generated from it nightly; a design edit that isn't in tokens.json doesn't exist.
        
            
        Hard-coded values fail CI. The lint rule velo/no-raw-dimen blocks hex literals and magic sizes outside the preview layer.
        
            
        Every component ships with its screenshot test at 3 widths × 2 themes × XL type. Visual diffs need two approvals.
        
            
        Debt is logged, not absorbed. If you deviate to hit a date, add a row to /docs/deviations.md with an owner and an expiry.
